Privacy Policy

Last updated — March 2026

Data Collection

Sakina does not collect, store, or share any personal data.

No accounts No sign-ins No analytics No tracking
Your Data Stays On-Device

The app runs entirely on your device. Your breathing preferences and settings are saved locally only and are never transmitted anywhere.

Health & Wellness Data

Sakina is designed to support breathing and relaxation. The app does not collect, store, or transmit any health or wellness data. Any breathing patterns or preferences you use within the app remain entirely on your device and are never shared with us or any third party.

Data Retention & Deletion

We do not store any personal data on external servers. Any preferences saved by the app are stored locally on your device only and are retained for as long as the app is installed.

To delete all app data, simply uninstall Sakina from your device. This will permanently remove all locally stored preferences. If you have any questions about data deletion, you can also contact us at tantonrory@gmail.com.

Advertising

The app displays ads via Google AdMob. Google may collect device identifiers and usage data in accordance with their own privacy policy to serve relevant ads.

You can review Google’s privacy policy at policies.google.com/privacy.

Contact

If you have any questions about this privacy policy, please reach out at tantonrory@gmail.com.
